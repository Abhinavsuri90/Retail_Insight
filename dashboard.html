Business Impact & Recommendations

Revenue Insights

  • Pareto Principle Validated: VIP and Elite segments (5% of customers) generate 47.7% of total revenue
  • High-Value Focus: 217 VIP/Elite customers contribute £4.2M in revenue
  • Cross-Sell Potential: 15-20% revenue increase possible through intelligent product bundling
  • ROI Impact: Cross-selling to a £10K customer yields £1K+ additional revenue

Strategic Recommendations

  • VIP Retention Program: Implement concierge service and exclusive access for top 0.3% customers
  • Elite Engagement: Premium product recommendations and early access programs for 4.7% Elite segment
  • Win-Back Campaigns: Targeted re-engagement for 70.4% At-Risk customers with >44 days inactivity
  • Product Bundling: Create "Regency Collection" bundles based on 21.3× lift associations
  • Differential Marketing: Allocate marketing resources proportional to customer lifetime value

Methodology & Technical Details

Machine Learning Approach

  • RFM Analysis: Calculated Recency, Frequency, Monetary metrics for each customer
  • K-Means Clustering: Applied with K=4 clusters (determined via Elbow Method)
  • Feature Scaling: StandardScaler for fair distance calculation
  • Apriori Algorithm: Min support 2%, min confidence 60%
  • Association Rules: Filtered for VIP/Elite segments to reduce noise

Data Quality

  • Dataset: UCI Online Retail II (541,909 raw transactions)
  • Cleaning: Removed nulls, negatives, outliers (73.4% retention rate)
  • Final Dataset: 397,885 transactions, 4,338 unique customers
  • Date Range: December 2010 - December 2011
  • Total Revenue: £8.9M analyzed
