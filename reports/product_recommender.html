How This Works

Step 1: Transaction Analysis

We analyzed 541,909 real customer transactions to find patterns in what products are bought together. This isn't guesswork - it's data science.

Step 2: FP-Growth Algorithm

Using advanced FP-Growth algorithm (faster than traditional Apriori), we discovered 243 frequent itemsets - combinations of products that appear together frequently.

Step 3: Association Rules

We generated 20 high-confidence rules with metrics:

  • Support: How often items appear together (2-3% of transactions)
  • Confidence: Probability of buying B given A (60-89%)
  • Lift: How much more likely than random (6-24× stronger)
Step 4: Smart Recommendations

When you search for a product, we instantly match it against our association rules and show you the strongest pairings. Products with lift > 15× are bundling opportunities. Products with 70%+ confidence are strong cross-sell candidates.
